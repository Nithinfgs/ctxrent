# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short:** be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work rather than the person, and don't harass, demean or exclude anyone. Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions and block participants who don't follow this.

**Reporting:** open a private security advisory on this repository or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
